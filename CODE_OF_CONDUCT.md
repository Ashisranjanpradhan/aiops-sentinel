# AIOps Sentinel Community Standards

The AIOps Sentinel project maintainers and contributors are committed to fostering a collaborative, professional, and supportive engineering environment.

## Guidelines
- **Professionalism:** Treat all contributors, reviewers, and users with respect, courtesy, and constructive communication.
- **Evidence-Based Discussion:** Focus technical discussions on code architecture, empirical metrics, system reliability, and reproducibility.
- **Constructive Review:** Deliver thoughtful, actionable feedback on pull requests and issues.
- **Privacy & Security:** Do not share personal information, confidential credentials, or proprietary cluster telemetry.

## Inquiries & Concerns
For inquiries or questions regarding community standards, contact the project maintainers at maintainers@aiops-sentinel.local.
