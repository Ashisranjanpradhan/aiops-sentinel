Scientific Rigor

Research Foundations & Empirical Benchmarks

Mathematical formulations, non-parametric statistical models, and reproducible evaluations on our internal synthetic cascading-failure benchmark.

1. Mathematical Formulations

Median Absolute Deviation (MAD)

A non-parametric measure of statistical dispersion resilient to extreme outliers in cloud-native workloads:

MAD = median(|X_i - median(X)|)
Modified Z-Score M_i = 0.6745 * (X_i - median(X)) / MAD

Where 0.6745 corresponds to the 75th percentile of the standard normal distribution, making modified Z-scores comparable to standard deviations for normal data.

Online EWMA Drift Detection

Maintains dynamic control limits over streaming metrics without unbounded memory accumulation:

S_t = α * Y_t + (1 - α) * S_(t-1)
UCL / LCL = μ_0 ± L * σ * sqrt((α / (2 - α)) * (1 - (1 - α)^(2t)))

With smoothing parameter α ∈ (0, 1] tuned to α = 0.2 in production streams to balance sensitivity against false positive alerts.

Isolation Forest Anomaly Scoring

Multi-variate tree isolation isolating anomalous multi-dimensional metric points with fewer partition splits:

s(x, n) = 2^(- E(h(x)) / c(n))
c(n) = 2 * (ln(n - 1) + 0.5772156649) - (2 * (n - 1) / n)

Instances with anomaly scores s → 1 are isolated as high-severity incidents; scores s < 0.5 denote nominal operations.

Topology-Aware Root Cause Ranking (Heuristic)

Ranks candidate root causes by traversing the microservice dependency topology backwards from observed symptom anomalies:

r^(k+1) = (1 - c) * P * r^(k) + c * e
Transition Probability P_ij = Corr(S_i, S_j) / Σ_k Corr(S_i, S_k)

Where c is the restart probability (0.15) and e is the seed vector of symptom anomalies. This structural ranking heuristic provides topology-aware prioritization, distinct from formal causal DAG discovery.

2. Empirical Evaluation on Internal Synthetic Cascading-Failure Benchmark

Evaluated on an internal synthetic cascading-failure benchmark consisting of 60 multi-service failure episodes (18 test episodes, seed=42) simulating realistic latency, error, and resource saturation cascades across a 7-service topology:

Evaluated Model / Approach Anomaly F1 Top-1 Accuracy Top-3 Accuracy MRR Methodological Characterization
Random Baseline 11.1% 33.3% 44.4% 0.495 Uniform random baseline establishing lower bound performance.
Isolation Forest (Unsupervised ML) 53.2% 11.1% 55.6% 0.405 Multi-variate tree isolation identifies anomalies but lacks topology graph context.
Max-Metric Heuristic 55.9% 100.0% 100.0% 1.000 High precision on dominant errors; low recall (38.8%) during gradual metric drift.
Online EWMA Drift + Topology Ranking 88.5% 100.0% 100.0% 1.000 Non-parametric statistical drift detection (98.2% recall) combined with topology ranking.
Benchmark Provenance & Future Evaluation: The above results reflect reproducible execution on the platform's internal cascading-failure dataset (generated via scripts/download-benchmarks.py). Formal evaluation against external published datasets, including the official RCAEval benchmark, represents an active engineering roadmap objective.
